[Intro]  
Dripping drops on the windowpane  
Rain is falling, let’s sing along  

[Verse 1]  
Puddles splash in the street,  
Raindrops dance on the beat.  

[Chorus]  
One, two, three, we wave and cheer,  
Four, five, six, the rain is here!  
Wave your hands like the wind can do,  
Seven, eight, nine, ten—say goodbye too!  

[Verse 2]  
Bare feet stomp in the mud,  
Raindrops laugh where the boots are stood.  

[Chorus]  
One, two, three, we wave and cheer,  
Four, five, six, the rain is here!  
Wave your hands like the wind can do,  
Seven, eight, nine, ten—say goodbye too!  

[Bridge]  
Raindrops fall, soft and slow,  
We count them as they go.  

[Final Chorus]  
One, two, three, we wave and cheer,  
Four, five, six, the rain is here!  
Wave your hands like the wind can do,  
Seven, eight, nine, ten—say goodbye too!  

[Outro]  
Rain stops falling, the sky is blue,  
We’ll remember the fun we had too.
