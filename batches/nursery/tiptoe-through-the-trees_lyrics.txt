[Intro]  
Gentle wind, a soft hello,  
Campers gather, you and me.  
Stars will shine, the fire will glow,  
Let’s explore the land with glee.  

[Verse 1]  
A tent is up, the ground is flat,  
We’re sleeping under moonlit stars.  
A stick and rope, we make a hat,  
Laughter echoes in the dark.  

[Chorus]  
Tiptoe, tiptoe, soft and light,  
Gentle steps in moonbeam night.  
Tiptoe, tiptoe, don’t you fall,  
Tiptoe through the trees, oh what a call!  
(Teachers: Lead children to tiptoe gently.)  

[Verse 2]  
A fire crackles, warm and bright,  
We sing and dance in campfire light.  
A bug buzzes, a frog says “ribbit,”  
We listen close, it’s getting quiet.  

[Chorus]  
Tiptoe, tiptoe, soft and light,  
Gentle steps in moonbeam night.  
Tiptoe, tiptoe, don’t you fall,  
Tiptoe through the trees, oh what a call!  
(Teachers: Lead children to tiptoe gently.)  

[Bridge]  
The forest whispers, calm and kind,  
We learn from trees, we learn from wind.  
The earth is full of wonder and grace,  
We take a breath, we hold it with care.  

[Final Chorus]  
Tiptoe, tiptoe, soft and light,  
Gentle steps in moonbeam night.  
Tiptoe, tiptoe, don’t you fall,  
Tiptoe through the trees, oh what a call!  
(Teachers: Lead children to tiptoe gently.)  

[Outro]  
The fire dies, the stars still gleam,  
We say goodbye, but not with a scream.  
We’ll come again, when skies are blue,  
Till then, we’ll dream of camp and you.
